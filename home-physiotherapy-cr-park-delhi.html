Understand Your Symptoms

Home Physiotherapy in CR Park, Delhi

Home physiotherapy may include assessment, guided exercises, mobility practice, functional training and recovery advice, depending on your condition and the equipment or support available at home.

Who May Enquire About Home Physiotherapy

People may enquire about home-based physiotherapy for mobility limitations, difficulty travelling, older-adult mobility support, recovery after surgery (with clearance), or rehabilitation needs that can safely be addressed at home. A clinician must assess suitability.

Symptoms do not always reveal the underlying cause. Assessment helps determine whether physiotherapy is appropriate or whether medical evaluation is needed.

When to Enquire About a Home Visit

You may contact the clinic to ask whether a home visit is suitable if you:

  • Pain, stiffness or difficulty moving a joint
  • Discomfort during walking, lifting, reaching or daily tasks
  • Movement limitations affecting work, home or recreational activities
  • Recovery support after an injury, procedure or clinician's recommendation

Can physiotherapy help with pain and movement difficulties?
Physiotherapy may help some people manage pain and movement concerns through graded mobility, strengthening and activity guidance. The appropriate approach depends on the cause and individual assessment.

Your physiotherapist will discuss your symptoms and relevant history, assess movement and function, and explain appropriate options. Further medical evaluation may be recommended when needed.
