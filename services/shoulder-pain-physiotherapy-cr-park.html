Understand Your Symptoms

What Is Shoulder Pain Physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy may help some people with shoulder pain by addressing mobility, strength, activity tolerance and self-management, depending on the cause and assessment findings.

Common Reasons for Shoulder Pain

Shoulder pain may be associated with muscle or tendon irritation, repetitive or overhead activity, joint problems, injury or other health conditions. It may involve pain when lifting the arm, stiffness, weakness or difficulty reaching, dressing or sleeping comfortably.

Symptoms alone cannot confirm the cause. A suitable assessment helps determine whether physiotherapy is appropriate or whether further medical evaluation is needed.

Symptoms We Can Discuss

Consider contacting the clinic for advice if you are experiencing concerns such as:

  • Shoulder stiffness or difficulty lifting or moving your arm
  • Pain during reaching, lifting or overhead activities
  • Shoulder and shoulder tension affecting daily activities
  • Shoulder symptoms that need assessment after an injury or clinician's advice
A Personalised Approach

How Physiotherapy May Support Shoulder Pain

Your care plan depends on your symptoms, examination findings, health history and goals. No single technique is suitable for every person.

Movement & Mobility

Where appropriate, your physiotherapist may guide graded movement exercises to support comfortable shoulder mobility.

Strength & Function

Individualised exercises may be recommended to build shoulder and surrounding muscle strength, coordination and tolerance for daily activities.

Posture & Activity Advice

Practical guidance may help you modify lifting, reaching, work habits and everyday activities while symptoms settle.

What to Expect

Your Shoulder Pain Care Journey

We focus on understanding your needs and agreeing on practical next steps with you.

Discuss Symptoms

Assessment

Review

Important: Seek urgent medical care for shoulder pain following a significant accident, or if you have new or worsening weakness, difficulty walking, loss of coordination, fever with severe headache or shoulder stiffness, or other sudden concerning symptoms. Physiotherapy should not delay urgent medical assessment.
Common Questions

FAQs About Shoulder Pain Treatment

Helpful information before contacting a physiotherapy clinic.

What can cause shoulder pain and stiffness?
Shoulder pain can arise from tendon or muscle irritation, repetitive or overhead activity, joint conditions, injury or other causes. A clinical assessment helps guide next steps; symptoms alone do not confirm a diagnosis.
Can physiotherapy help with shoulder discomfort?
Physiotherapy may help some people manage shoulder pain through graded mobility, strengthening and activity guidance. The appropriate approach depends on the cause and individual assessment.
What happens during the first appointment?
Your physiotherapist will ask about symptoms and relevant history, assess suitable shoulder movement and function, and explain appropriate options. Further medical evaluation may be recommended when needed.
How many sessions will I need for shoulder pain?
Session needs vary with the cause and duration of symptoms, assessment findings, goals and response to treatment. Your physiotherapist can discuss expectations after assessment; a fixed outcome or session count cannot be guaranteed.
Do you provide shoulder pain physiotherapy in CR Park?
Gentle Hands PhysioCare is in Chittaranjan Park, South Delhi. Call +91 99271 50034 to confirm the clinic address, appointment availability and whether the service is suitable.
Can I manage shoulder pain at home?
Advice depends on the cause and symptoms. Temporarily modify activities that aggravate pain and seek professional guidance if symptoms persist or limit daily life. Avoid forceful manipulation or exercises that worsen symptoms without clinical advice.
